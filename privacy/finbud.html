Privacy Policy — FinBud

Package name: com.finbud · View on Google Play

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed FinBud ("the App"), a multilingual personal finance manager, expense tracker, and budgeting tool. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Because FinBud handles your financial records, we've written this policy to be specific about that data.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

You can use FinBud as a Guest without creating an account, or sign in with your Google Account. If you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and profile picture) from Google to identify your account and enable sync across devices.

Financial data you enter

  • Transactions you record (amounts, dates, descriptions, and custom categories or icons you assign).
  • Budgets and spending targets you set.
  • Any notes or labels you add to organize your finances.

FinBud does not connect to your bank accounts, cards, or financial institutions, and does not collect bank login credentials. All financial data is entered manually by you.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language, and crash/diagnostic logs, used to keep the App stable.
  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) — used by our ad partner to serve and measure ads, and to limit how often you see the same ad, in the free version of the App.

2. Sync & Data Storage

If you sign in with Google, your transactions, budgets, and categories are encrypted and synced to secure cloud storage so you can access them across your devices. If you use FinBud as a Guest, your data is stored only locally on your device and is not backed up by us — uninstalling the App or clearing its storage will permanently delete Guest data.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App displays ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use the advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your advertising ID from your device's Settings → Google → Ads menu. For more information, see Google's Advertising Policy. We do not use your financial transaction data to target ads.

4. Permissions This App May Request

PermissionPurpose
Internet / Network stateSync your data with your account and serve ads (free version).
Google Sign-InOptional — used only if you choose to sign in for cross-device sync and backup.

The exact permissions requested may vary by Android version and app update; you can review and revoke them at any time in your device's App Settings.

5. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features (recording transactions, budgets, categorization, and optional cross-device sync).
  • To authenticate your account if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability and performance.
  • To serve and measure advertising that supports the free version of the App.

We do not sell your personal or financial information, and we do not share your individual transaction data with advertisers.

6. Data Security

Data synced to the cloud is encrypted in transit and at rest. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your Google Account credentials secure.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

If you sign in with a Google Account, your synced data is retained until you delete individual records within the App, delete your account, or contact us to request deletion. Guest data stored only on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal and financial data, and to opt out of personalized advertising. You can manage most of your data directly within the App, or contact us using the details below to request access, export, or deletion of your account data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at awestrucktech@gmail.com.
