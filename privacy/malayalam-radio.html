Privacy Policy — Malayalam Radio FM Songs

Package name: malayalam.radio.fm.songs.malayalamradio · View on Google Play

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed Malayalam Radio FM Songs ("the App") as a free, ad-supported live radio streaming app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, sign-in, or registration, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your favourite stations (with the date you added them), your last few recently played stations, your theme choice and other settings are stored only on your device.

Information collected automatically

The App uses the following third-party services, which collect data automatically:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase (usage analytics).
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting).
  • Firebase Remote Config (remote settings).
  • Google AdMob with its Meta Audience Network mediation partner (advertising).

Together they may collect:

  • Identifiers: the Google Advertising ID and other device identifiers, plus a random app-instance ID and installation ID created by Firebase. They are used to serve and measure ads, count users, limit ad repetition, and prevent fraud.
  • In-app activity: the stations you play, and the stations you add to or remove from favourites (including the station name). It also covers sleep-timer use, how long audio takes to start, rating-prompt responses, and ad events such as which ads were shown or skipped.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: crash reports and error logs, which may include the name of the station that was playing when the error occurred. Also included are device model, operating system version, app version, language and network state.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address, used for regional statistics and ad relevance, not precise tracking.
  • Install source: how the App was installed (for example from Google Play), via the Play Install Referrer service.

This data is not linked to your name, email address or phone number. We use it in aggregate to understand which stations and features people use, fix crashes and playback problems, and tune how often ads appear. Searching for stations happens on your device, and your search terms are not sent anywhere.

2. Station Directory

The station list is loaded from the open, community-maintained Radio Browser directory, filtered to Malayalam-language stations and cached on your device for about a day. Station logos are loaded from the web addresses each station publishes. When the App fetches the list or logos, those servers receive standard request information such as your IP address. No personal information or listening history is sent to the directory.

3. Radio Streams & Third-Party Broadcasters

The App streams live audio from independently operated Malayalam FM and internet radio stations (such as Club FM, Radio Mirchi, Kochi FM, and other regional broadcasters) by connecting directly to each station's publicly available streaming server. Song and show titles shown in the App come from information inside the station's own stream. Those servers are run by the broadcasters themselves and can see standard connection information such as your IP address. To hide stations that are offline, the App also briefly checks station streams in the background about once a day. Some stations only offer unencrypted (HTTP) streams. We do not host or record this audio, and each broadcaster's own privacy practices apply to the stream itself; we are not responsible for their data practices. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by any radio station or broadcaster.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob, with Meta Audience Network taking part as a mediation (bidding) partner. Ad formats include:

  • Banners on the Now Playing and Search screens.
  • Native ads that appear among stations.
  • Occasional full-screen ads when you return to the App after a while, or finish with a screen.

Full-screen ads are not shown during your first day with the App or while audio is playing, and are limited per day. We adjust these limits remotely using Firebase Remote Config.

AdMob, Meta, and their partners may use the advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. On supported Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (Topics and Attribution Reporting).

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and for certain US states), the App first shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only as your choice allows. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App. You can also opt out of personalized advertising, manage ad topics, or reset or delete your advertising ID from your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) menu. For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Meta's Privacy Policy.

5. Other Features

  • Android Auto, the home-screen widget and the Quick Settings tile let you play stations without opening the App. Pressing play on Bluetooth headphones or the notification can resume your last station.
  • Sleep timer stops playback after the time you choose.
  • Share and rate hand a Play Store link to the app you choose, or open the App's Google Play listing.

7. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features (station browsing and search, playback, favourites, recently played, sleep timer, widget, Android Auto, sharing, light/dark theme).
  • To understand how the App is used and which stations are popular, so we can improve it (Firebase Analytics).
  • To find and fix crashes and playback errors (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • To serve and measure advertising that keeps the App free, and to adjust ad frequency (AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Firebase Remote Config).

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Storage, Retention & Security

Your favourites, recently played stations, settings, and the cached station list are stored locally on your device and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. The App's data is excluded from Android cloud backup. We do not operate servers that store your data. Analytics and crash data are processed and stored by Google (Firebase) on our behalf and kept according to Firebase's retention settings. We see this data only as aggregated reports and individual crash reports, and it does not include your name or contact details. Data sent to Google and Meta is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled under their respective privacy policies: the Google Privacy Policy (see also Firebase privacy information) and Meta's Privacy Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Live radio is unfiltered, third-party content. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the App has no accounts, the data described above is tied only to random identifiers, not to your identity. You can:

  • Change your ad consent in the App's settings, where offered.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Clear the App's data or reinstall it, which also resets the Firebase app-instance ID and starts a new, unlinked history.
  • Revoke notification permission.

For data held by Google, use Google My Ad Center. You can also contact us using the details below.
