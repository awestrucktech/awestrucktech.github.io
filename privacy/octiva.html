Privacy Policy — Octiva

Package name: com.pdf.doc.octiva

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed Octiva ("the App"), a free, ad-supported document reader and PDF toolkit for Android. Octiva opens PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, image and text files, and lets you scan, annotate, redact, merge, split, compress, sign and convert them. Your documents are processed on your device and are never uploaded to us or to any server. This Privacy Policy explains what the App does and does not collect, and the choices you have.

1. Your Documents Stay on Your Device

Every document feature in Octiva, including viewing, editing, annotation, form filling, redaction, OCR (text recognition), page tools, compression, conversion, comparison and read-aloud, runs locally on your phone. We do not operate servers that receive your files, scans, photos, text or the results of any tool. The App has no account, sign-in or registration, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To work properly, the App stores the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Settings and preferences, such as theme, language, reader options, save location and onboarding state.
  • Recent files, bookmarks and a scan index (the names and locations of files you opened or created, and page bookmarks).
  • Private working copies. When you open a document from another app (for example by choosing "Open with Octiva" in a messaging, email or file app), Android only grants temporary access. So that the file can be reopened from Recents later, the App keeps a private copy inside its own storage. These copies are not accessible to other apps and are removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it.
  • Files you create. Output files are saved to the location you choose (by default your Downloads/Octiva folder) using Android's file picker and media store. You decide the file name before it is saved.

None of this information is sent to us. We have no way to access, view or recover it.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App contains Google software that may transmit limited technical data. This data does not include the contents of your documents.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Octiva is free because it shows ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are shown only on selected screens such as Settings and, occasionally, after a task completes; they are never shown inside the document viewer, editors or the scanner. AdMob and its partners may collect and use:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers, to serve and measure ads, limit repetition and prevent fraud.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, IP address and diagnostic data.
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address, for regional ad relevance.
  • App interactions, such as ad impressions and taps.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and for certain US states), the App first shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only as your choice allows. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy or ad settings option in the App's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Google ML Kit and Google Play services

Octiva uses Google ML Kit and Google Play services for the document scanner, QR and barcode scanner, on-device text recognition (OCR) and automatic background removal. The images and text are processed on your device. Google may collect usage, performance and diagnostic information from these libraries (for example, API usage, latency, crash and device information) under the Google Privacy Policy. Some features, such as background removal, may require Google Play services to download a model the first time; that download is performed by Google, and no document content is sent.

4. Features That Use the Internet

Apart from the advertising and Google services above, the App connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

  • Web to PDF. If you enter a web address to convert a page to PDF, the App loads that address, so the website receives standard connection information such as your IP address, and the site's own privacy practices apply. We do not receive the address or the page.
  • Links. Opening a link inside a document, or sharing a file, hands it to your browser or the app you pick.

6. How We Use Information

  • To provide the document features you choose to use, entirely on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the App free (through Google AdMob, as described above).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your documents for advertising, analytics, profiling or training any model.

7. Data Security and Retention

Your files and settings live in your device's storage and in locations you choose, and they are protected by your device's own security. Data sent by Google services travels over encrypted connections. We keep no copy of your data. Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it removes the App's private data, recents and working copies; files you saved to Downloads or elsewhere remain until you delete them. If you use the App's encryption tool, remember your password: we cannot recover it or the file.

8. Children's Privacy

Octiva is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad or other service in the App, contact us so we can help.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and the Australian Privacy Act 1988), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because we hold no personal data about you, these choices are made mainly on your device: change your ad consent in the App's settings, reset your advertising ID, revoke permissions, or clear the App's data. For data held by Google, use Google My Ad Center. You may also contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if a future version adds a feature or service that changes how data is handled. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page, and we will update this policy before any change takes effect.
