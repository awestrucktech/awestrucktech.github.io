Privacy Policy — Hindi Radio FM Songs

Package name: hindi.radio.fm.songs.hindiradio · View on Google Play

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed Hindi Radio FM Songs ("the App") as a free, ad-supported live radio streaming app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, sign-in, or registration, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your favourite stations (with the date you added them), your last few recently played stations, your theme choice and other settings are stored only on your device. Searching for stations happens on your device, and your search terms are not sent anywhere.

Information collected automatically

The App itself does not include analytics or crash-reporting services. The third-party SDKs that collect data are Google AdMob and its Meta Audience Network mediation partner, which are used to show ads (see section 4). They may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers: used to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent ad fraud.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and ad-related diagnostic and crash data.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address, used for regional ad relevance, not precise tracking.
  • App interactions: such as ad views and taps, used for advertising and ad performance measurement.

2. Station Directory

The station list is loaded from the open, community-maintained Radio Browser directory, filtered to Hindi-language stations and cached on your device for about a day. Station logos are loaded from the web addresses each station publishes. When the App fetches the list or logos, those servers receive standard request information such as your IP address. No personal information or listening history is sent to the directory.

3. Radio Streams & Third-Party Broadcasters

The App streams live audio from independently operated Hindi and Bollywood FM and internet radio stations by connecting directly to each station's publicly available streaming server. Song and show titles shown in the App come from information inside the station's own stream. Those servers are run by the broadcasters themselves and can see standard connection information such as your IP address. To hide stations that are offline, the App also briefly checks station streams in the background about once a day. Some stations only offer unencrypted (HTTP) streams. We do not host or record this audio, and each broadcaster's own privacy practices apply to the stream itself; we are not responsible for their data practices. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by any radio station or broadcaster.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob, with Meta Audience Network taking part as a mediation (bidding) partner. Ad formats include:

  • Banners on the Now Playing and Search screens.
  • Native ads that appear among stations.
  • Occasional full-screen ads when you return to the App after a while, or finish with a screen.

Full-screen ads are not shown during your first day with the App or while audio is playing, and are limited per day.

AdMob, Meta, and their partners may use the advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. On supported Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (Topics and Attribution Reporting).

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and for certain US states), the App first shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only as your choice allows. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App. You can also opt out of personalized advertising, manage ad topics, or reset or delete your advertising ID from your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) menu. For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Meta's Privacy Policy.

5. Other Features

  • Android Auto, the home-screen widget and the Quick Settings tile let you play stations without opening the App. Pressing play on Bluetooth headphones or the notification can resume your last station.
  • Sleep timer stops playback after the time you choose.
  • Share and rate hand a Play Store link to the app you choose, or open the App's Google Play listing.

7. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features (station browsing and search, playback, favourites, recently played, sleep timer, widget, Android Auto, sharing, light/dark theme).
  • To serve and measure advertising that keeps the App free.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Storage, Retention & Security

Your favourites, recently played stations, settings, and the cached station list are stored locally on your device and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. The App's data is excluded from Android cloud backup. We do not operate servers that store your data. Data sent to AdMob and Meta is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled under their respective privacy policies: the Google Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Live radio is unfiltered, third-party content. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the App has no accounts, the data described above is tied only to random identifiers, not to your identity. You can:

  • Change your ad consent in the App's settings, where offered.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Clear the App's data.
  • Revoke notification permission.

For data held by Google, use Google My Ad Center. You can also contact us using the details below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features or change the services the App uses. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
