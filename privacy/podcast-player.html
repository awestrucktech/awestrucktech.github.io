Privacy Policy — Awestruck Podcast Player

Package name: awestruck.podcast.podcastplayer · View on Google Play

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed the Awestruck Podcast Player app ("the App") as a free, ad-supported podcast discovery and playback app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, sign-in, or registration, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your subscriptions, episode history and playback positions, play queue, downloaded episodes, transcripts, and settings are stored only on your device. If you import or export an OPML file of subscriptions, you choose the file using Android's file picker or share sheet, and we never see its contents.

Information collected automatically

The App itself does not include analytics or crash-reporting services. The only third-party SDK that collects data is Google AdMob, which is used to show ads (see section 4). AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers: used to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent ad fraud.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and ad-related diagnostic and crash data.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address, used for regional ad relevance, not precise tracking.
  • App interactions: such as ad views and taps, used for advertising and ad performance measurement.

Your search and listening activity is not sent to us. Search terms go only to the podcast directory you use (see section 2).

2. Podcast Search & Content

To find shows, the App queries a third-party podcast directory: iTunes/Apple Podcasts by default, or PodcastIndex.org if you select it in Settings. It fetches episode audio, artwork, show notes, chapters, and transcripts directly from the RSS feeds and hosting servers published by each podcast's creator. Your search terms and feed requests are sent to these providers and hosts. Like any web request, they can see your IP address and basic device information (such as your Android version). We do not control their data practices. When you share an episode, the link points to the pod.link sharing service. Downloaded episodes are saved in the App's own storage folder for offline playback and are removed when you delete the download, clear the App's data, or uninstall it.

3. Background Refresh, Notifications & Other Features

  • Background refresh (off by default): checks your subscribed feeds for new episodes periodically, including after the device restarts.
  • Notifications: playback controls, download progress, and an optional library-sync notice.
  • Android Auto: lets you browse and play podcasts from your car's display.
  • Subscribe links: podcast "subscribe" links can open directly in the App.
  • Ratings: the App may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google handles any rating you leave.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob. These include banners at the bottom of the screen and within lists, occasional full-screen ads when you open a podcast, and an ad when you return to the App. Full-screen ads are not shown during your first few days with the App, are spaced minutes apart, and never interrupt audio that is playing. AdMob and its partners may use the advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. On supported Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (Topics and Attribution Reporting). You can opt out of personalized advertising, manage ad topics, or reset or delete your advertising ID from your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) menu. For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features (search, playback, downloads, subscriptions, queue, transcripts, background refresh, Android Auto, OPML import/export, sharing).
  • To serve and measure advertising that keeps the App free.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your subscriptions, history, downloads, and settings are stored locally on your device and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. If Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's settings and library in your device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup, and we cannot access it. We do not operate servers that store your data. Data collected by AdMob is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled under Google's privacy policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Podcasts are unfiltered, third-party content. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the App has no accounts and we store no personal data on our side, most of these choices are made directly on your device: reset or delete your advertising ID, revoke permissions, or clear the App's data. For data held by Google, use Google My Ad Center. You can also contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add analytics or crash reporting to the App. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at awestrucktech@gmail.com.
