Privacy Policy — Australia FM Radio

Package name: australia.radio.fm.songs.australiaradio · View on Google Play

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed Australia FM Radio ("the App") as a free, ad-supported app for streaming live Australian FM, AM and internet radio stations, and for discovering, playing and downloading podcasts. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, sign-in, or registration, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The following are stored on your device (see section 9):

  • Your favourite and recently played stations.
  • Podcast subscriptions, episode progress and played status, your Up Next queue and downloaded episodes.
  • Theme choice and other settings.

If you import or export an OPML file of podcast subscriptions, you choose the file using Android's file picker, and we never see its contents.

Information collected automatically

The App uses the following third-party services, which collect data automatically:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase (usage analytics).
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting).
  • Firebase Remote Config (remote settings).
  • Google AdMob with its Meta Audience Network mediation partner (advertising).

Together they may collect:

  • Identifiers: the Google Advertising ID and other device identifiers, plus a random app-instance ID and installation ID created by Firebase. They are used to serve and measure ads, count users, limit ad repetition, and prevent fraud.
  • In-app activity: screens viewed, and stations you play, favourite or report as not working (including the station name). Also podcasts you open, subscribe to or play (including podcast and episode titles), search terms you type, and use of features such as the sleep timer, playback speed and OPML import/export. It also includes ad events such as ad requests, impressions, taps and estimated ad revenue.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: crash reports and error logs, which may include the name of the station or episode that was playing when the error occurred. Also included are device model, operating system version, app version, language and network state.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address, used for regional statistics and ad relevance, not precise tracking.
  • Install source: how the App was installed (for example from Google Play), via the Play Install Referrer service.

This data is not linked to your name, email address or phone number. We use it in aggregate to understand which features and content people use, fix crashes, and tune how often ads appear.

2. Station Directory

The radio station list is loaded from the open, community-maintained Radio Browser directory, filtered to Australian stations and cached on your device. Station logos are loaded from the web addresses each station publishes. When the App fetches the list or logos, those servers receive standard request information such as your IP address. No personal information or listening history is sent to the directory. Searching for stations happens on your device.

3. Podcast Search & Content

To find shows and show top charts, the App queries Apple's iTunes/Apple Podcasts directory and chart feeds, so podcast search terms you enter are sent to Apple. The App fetches episode audio, artwork, show notes and chapters directly from the RSS feed and hosting servers published by each podcast's creator. These providers and hosts receive standard request information such as your IP address. We do not control their data practices. By default, the App also refreshes your subscribed feeds in the background every few hours, so it can show new episodes and, if you allow notifications, tell you about them. You can turn this off in Settings. Downloaded episodes are saved in the App's own storage folder for offline playback, and removed when you delete the download, clear the App's data, or uninstall it.

4. Radio Streams & Third-Party Broadcasters

The App streams live audio by connecting directly to each station's publicly available streaming server. Those servers are run by the broadcasters themselves and can see standard connection information such as your IP address. To hide stations that are offline, the App also briefly checks station streams in the background about once a day. Some stations and podcast hosts only offer unencrypted (HTTP) connections. We do not host or record this audio, and each broadcaster's own privacy practices apply to its stream; we are not responsible for their data practices. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by any radio station, broadcaster, or podcast creator.

5. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob, with Meta Audience Network taking part as a mediation (bidding) partner. Ad formats include:

  • Banners at the bottom of the screen, under the player and within lists.
  • Sponsored (native) listings on the Home screen.
  • Occasional full-screen ads when you return to the App or finish with a screen.

Full-screen ads are not shown during your first day with the App or while audio is playing, and are limited per day. We adjust these limits remotely using Firebase Remote Config. AdMob, Meta, and their partners may use the advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. On supported Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (Topics and Attribution Reporting).

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and for certain US states), the App first shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only as your choice allows. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the App. You can also opt out of personalized advertising, manage ad topics, or reset or delete your advertising ID from your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) menu. For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Meta's Privacy Policy.

6. Other Features

  • Android Auto, Google Assistant and the home-screen widget / Quick Settings tile let you browse and play stations and podcasts. Requests such as "play [station]" are handled by Google Assistant under Google's privacy policy.
  • Sharing sends a station name and Play Store link, or an episode's title, show and audio link, to the app you choose.
  • Rate and feedback open the Google Play listing or your email app; nothing is sent unless you send it.

8. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features (station and podcast browsing and search, playback, downloads, favourites, recents and subscriptions, background feed refresh, Android Auto, OPML import/export, sharing, light/dark theme).
  • To understand how the App is used and which stations and podcasts are popular, so we can improve it (Firebase Analytics).
  • To find and fix crashes and playback errors (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • To serve and measure advertising that keeps the App free, and to adjust ad frequency (AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Firebase Remote Config).

We do not sell your personal information.

9. Data Storage, Retention & Security

Your favourites, recents, subscriptions, queue, downloaded episodes, settings, and the cached station and podcast data are stored locally on your device. They are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. The App's data is excluded from Android cloud backup. Analytics and crash data are processed and stored by Google (Firebase) on our behalf and kept according to Firebase's retention settings. We see this data only as aggregated reports and individual crash reports, and it does not include your name or contact details. Data sent to Google and Meta is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled under their respective privacy policies: the Google Privacy Policy (see also Firebase privacy information) and Meta's Privacy Policy.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Live radio and podcasts are unfiltered, third-party content. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. You may also have the right to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the App has no accounts, the data described above is tied only to random identifiers, not to your identity. You can:

  • Change your ad consent in the App's settings, where offered.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Clear the App's data or reinstall it, which resets the Firebase app-instance ID and starts a new, unlinked history.
  • Revoke notification permission.
  • Turn off background refresh.

For data held by Google, use Google My Ad Center. You can also contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add features or change the services the App uses. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
