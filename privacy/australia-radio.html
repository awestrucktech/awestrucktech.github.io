Privacy Policy — Australia FM Radio

Package name: australia.radio.fm.songs.australiaradio · View on Google Play

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed Australia FM Radio ("the App") as a free, ad-supported app for streaming live Australian FM, AM and internet radio stations, and for discovering, playing and downloading podcasts. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, sign-in, or registration, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your favourite stations, podcast subscriptions, downloaded episodes, playback position, theme choice, and other settings are stored only on your device. If you import or export an OPML file of podcast subscriptions, that file is read from or written to a location you choose using Android's file picker — we never see its contents.

Information collected automatically

The App itself does not include analytics or crash-reporting services. The third-party SDKs that collect data are Google AdMob and its Meta Audience Network mediation partner, which are used to show ads (see section 5). They may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers — used to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent ad fraud.
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, app version, language, and ad-related diagnostic and crash data.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address, used for regional ad relevance, not precise tracking.
  • App interactions — such as ad views and taps, used for advertising and ad performance measurement.

Search terms you enter are sent to the relevant directory or provider described in sections 2 and 3 to return results; we do not otherwise collect or store your search or listening activity on our own servers.

2. Station Directory

The radio station list is loaded from the open, community-maintained Radio Browser directory, filtered to Australian stations and cached on your device. When the App fetches the list, the directory's servers receive standard request information such as your IP address. No personal information or listening history is sent to it.

3. Podcast Search & Content

To find shows, the App queries third-party podcast directories (iTunes/Apple Podcasts and PodcastIndex.org) and fetches episode audio, artwork, show notes, chapters, and transcripts directly from the RSS feed and hosting servers published by each podcast's creator. Your search terms and feed requests are sent to these providers and hosts to return results and content; we do not control their data practices. Downloaded episodes are saved as files in the App's own storage on your device for offline playback, and removed when you delete the download, clear the App's data, or uninstall it.

4. Radio Streams & Third-Party Broadcasters

The App streams live audio by connecting directly to each station's publicly available streaming server. Those servers are run by the broadcasters themselves and can see standard connection information such as your IP address. We do not host or record this audio, and each broadcaster's own privacy practices apply to its stream; we are not responsible for their data practices. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by any radio station, broadcaster, or podcast creator.

5. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob, with Meta Audience Network taking part as a mediation (bidding) partner. AdMob, Meta, and their partners may use the advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset or delete your advertising ID from your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) menu. For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Meta's Privacy Policy.

7. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features (station and podcast browsing and search, playback, downloads, favourites and subscriptions, OPML import/export, sharing, light/dark theme).
  • To serve and measure advertising that keeps the App free.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Storage & Security

Your favourites, subscriptions, downloaded episodes, settings, and the cached station and podcast data are stored locally on your device and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. We do not operate servers that store your data. Data collected by AdMob and Meta Audience Network is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled under their respective privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Live radio and podcasts are unfiltered, third-party content. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the App has no accounts and we store no personal data on our side, most of these choices are made directly in your device settings: reset or delete your advertising ID, revoke permissions, or clear the App's data. You can also contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add analytics or crash reporting to the App. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at awestrucktech@gmail.com.
