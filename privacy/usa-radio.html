Privacy Policy — USA FM Radio

Package name: usa.radio.fm.songs.usaradio · View on Google Play

Awestruck Tech ("we", "us", "our") developed USA FM Radio ("the App") as a free, ad-supported app for streaming live US FM, AM and internet radio stations, and for discovering, playing and downloading podcasts. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

The App has no accounts, sign-in, or registration, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The following are stored only on your device:

  • Your favorite and recently played stations.
  • Podcast subscriptions, episode history and playback positions, play queue, downloaded episodes and transcripts.
  • Theme choice and other settings.

If you import or export an OPML file of podcast subscriptions, you choose the file using Android's file picker or share sheet, and we never see its contents.

Information collected automatically

The App itself does not include analytics or crash-reporting services. The third-party SDKs that collect data are Google AdMob and its Meta Audience Network mediation partner, which are used to show ads (see section 6). They may collect:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers: used to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent ad fraud.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and ad-related diagnostic and crash data.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address, used for regional ad relevance, not precise tracking.
  • App interactions: such as ad views and taps, used for advertising and ad performance measurement.

Search terms you enter go only to the directory or provider described in sections 2 and 3, to return results. We do not otherwise collect or store your search or listening activity on our own servers.

2. Station Directory

The station list is loaded from the open, community-maintained Radio Browser directory, filtered to US stations and cached on your device. Station logos are loaded from the web addresses each station publishes. Both the directory and the logo hosts receive standard request information such as your IP address. No personal information or listening history is sent to them.

3. Podcast Search & Content

To find shows, the App queries a third-party podcast directory: iTunes/Apple Podcasts by default, or PodcastIndex.org if you select it in Settings. It fetches episode audio, artwork, show notes, chapters, and transcripts directly from the RSS feed and hosting servers published by each podcast's creator. Your search terms and feed requests are sent to these providers and hosts. Like any web request, they can see your IP address and basic device information (such as your Android version). We do not control their data practices. When you share an episode, the link points to the pod.link sharing service. Downloaded episodes are saved in the App's own storage folder for offline playback and are removed when you delete the download, clear the App's data, or uninstall it.

4. Radio Streams & Third-Party Broadcasters

The App streams live audio by connecting directly to each station's publicly available streaming server. Those servers are run by the broadcasters themselves and can see standard connection information such as your IP address. Some stations only offer unencrypted (HTTP) streams. We do not host or record this audio, and each broadcaster's own privacy practices apply to its stream; we are not responsible for their data practices. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by any radio station, broadcaster, or podcast creator.

5. Other Features

  • Background refresh (off by default): checks your subscribed podcasts for new episodes periodically, including after the device restarts.
  • Notifications: playback controls, download progress, and optional new-episode and library-sync alerts (off by default).
  • Home-screen widget: shows the name of the station you last played.
  • Android Auto: lets you browse and play stations and podcasts from your car's display.
  • Fonts: the App may download its display font from Google Fonts the first time it is needed, so Google receives standard request information such as your IP address.
  • Ratings & feedback: the App may occasionally ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google handles any rating you leave. If you choose to send feedback, it goes through your own email or browser app.

6. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob, with Meta Audience Network taking part as a mediation (bidding) partner. Ad formats include:

  • Banners at the bottom of the screen, within lists and on the player screens.
  • Native ads that appear among stations and podcasts.
  • Occasional full-screen ads when you open a podcast or search result.
  • An ad when you return to the App.

Full-screen ads are not shown during your first few days with the App, are spaced minutes apart, and never interrupt audio that is playing. AdMob, Meta, and their partners may use the advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. On supported Android versions, the ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox features (Topics and Attribution Reporting). You can opt out of personalized advertising, manage ad topics, or reset or delete your advertising ID from your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) menu. For more information, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Meta's Privacy Policy.

8. How We Use Information

  • To operate core features (station and podcast browsing and search, playback, downloads, favorites, recents and subscriptions, background refresh, home-screen widget, Android Auto, OPML import/export, sharing, light/dark theme).
  • To serve and measure advertising that keeps the App free.

We do not sell your personal information.

9. Data Storage & Security

Your favorites, recents, subscriptions, downloads, settings, and the cached station and podcast data are stored locally on your device and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. If Google backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's settings and library in your device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup, and we cannot access it. We do not operate servers that store your data. Data collected by AdMob and Meta Audience Network is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled under their respective privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Live radio and podcasts are unfiltered, third-party content. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live (including under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and other US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because the App has no accounts and we store no personal data on our side, most of these choices are made directly on your device: reset or delete your advertising ID, revoke permissions, or clear the App's data. For data held by Google, use Google My Ad Center. You can also contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add analytics or crash reporting to the App. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at awestrucktech@gmail.com.
